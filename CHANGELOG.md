# Changelog

## 0.10.0 — generating functions and specified binary64 execution

Cancellation-aware rational OGF compiler with minimal sequence witnesses, exact recurrence/state extraction, arithmetic-progression queries and supported Cauchy/binomial remainders. Portable integer binary64 decoding, rounding, exact dot products and stored-value power decisions. Uniform matrix-loading and execution-error bounds for a guarded round-once runtime compose with finite-model and infinite-source packets. Eight new CLI kinds, eight specs and deterministic receipts. The execution guarantee is finite-horizon and protocol-specific; no hardware speedup or arbitrary BLAS certification is claimed.

## 0.9.0 — infinite spectral sources

Explicit Ramanujan logarithmic moments, rational power-moment families and an infinite heat rod compile to rational modal dynamics with all-time omitted-mode and coefficient budgets. All-time selected-mode compression and arbitrary finite-horizon composition account for both stages. Outward rational special-function enclosures, two-cusp continued-fraction evaluation, guarded exact execution, four CLI kinds, six specs and a reproducible analytic-source demonstration are added. All 188 tests pass with numerical extras; 170 pass and eighteen skip without them. No generic infinite-source, intersample projection or physical-calibration claim is implied.

## 0.8.0 — stable discovery and fast rigorous acceptance

Lyapunov-coordinate proposal context with one metric preparation and exact reduced Euclidean contraction; outward-dyadic finite-horizon box brackets for arbitrary encoders/reduced dynamics, attained lower witnesses, adaptive precision and cached source pullbacks; independently replayed prepared execution. Pinned external comparison includes matched-tolerance balanced search, restored building acceptance, full startup costs and rejected candidates. All 159 tests pass; the complete monograph incorporates the new derivations.

## 0.7.1 — external engineering-model benchmark

Pinned SLICOT heat, PDE and building models; rational box acceptance and independent replay of six compressed packets; full and balanced-truncation baselines at ranks four, eight and twelve; retained failures, raw timings, cold-start costs and acquisition hashes. All-time v0.7 work is incorporated into the complete monograph. All 148 tests pass.

## 0.7.0 — 10 October 2026

- `forever`: all-time output-error brackets for persistent input boxes. Sharp dyadic sums with rounding tracked in the dual norm of a certified metric, a geometric tail, and an attained lower bound that can refute a tolerance. Also steady-state moment directions and all-time enrichment.
- `stability`: exact Schur–Cohn and Cayley/Hurwitz decisions; rational quadratic Lyapunov certificates for discrete and continuous time with near-spectral contraction factors; common quadratic certificates for switching.
- `zonotope`: sharp error suprema over correlated scenario domains whose generators couple coordinates and times, with attaining scenarios.
- `examples/thermal_forever.py`: the nine-state twenty-step plate model is refuted at t = 21 with all-time worst error ≈ 3.26665; a seven-state model is certified below 0.1 for all time.
- New CLI kinds `stability`, `common_quadratic`, `all_time` and `zonotope`, and five new example specifications. The suite grows from 131 to 148 methods; every earlier receipt regenerates unchanged.

## 0.6.0 — 10 October 2026

Sharp box acceptance under arbitrary time-varying convex mode uncertainty. Commuting full/projected families compress words through a count-lattice signed-support dynamic program; noncommuting families use complete budgeted enumeration. Exact common closure bypasses word search. `PreparedSwitching` replays once, supports shared initial fields, and checks rational box membership through directed binary64 endpoints. New `switching_enrichment` CLI kind and switching scenario application, with three production benchmarks including construction/preparation amortization. The suite grows from 117 to 131 methods.

## 0.5.0 — 10 October 2026

Sharp box-uniform general-direction enrichment with exact output-error kernels and attaining counterexamples; sparse coefficient-family closure; sharp full-model output ranges; thermal scenario workbench and optional batched QR execution with explicit rounding limits. Scenario-bound propagation now caches target pullbacks instead of full matrix powers, preserving earlier receipts. New CLI kinds `uniform_enrichment` and `sparse_family`, and command `dynacomp-thermal`. The suite grows from 95 to 117 methods.

## 0.4.0 — 10 October 2026

- `realization`: minimal input-output realization for zero initial state, with a Hankel-block minimality certificate checked against the original Markov parameters.
- `switched`: closure under every mode sequence, with experiment-labelled encoder rows; weighted automaton and HMM minimization (Schützenberger) with a Hankel certificate; equivalence checking that returns a shortest distinguishing word or an invariant-span certificate.
- `parametric` and `upoly`: generic observable, reachable and minimal orders for one real parameter, plus exact rank-drop loci (Cauchy–Binet, Faddeev–LeVerrier, Sturm isolation, rational roots found without factoring).
- `enrich`: certified greedy enrichment of approximate linear reductions to a target tolerance on supplied scenarios.
- `polynomial`: controlled polynomial closure, producing exact lifts z' = Σ uᵐ G_m z.
- `krylov`: a labelled breadth-first Krylov span with an incremental echelon form. The sparse compiler now uses it, roughly 28× faster on a 256-state stencil, and the 0.1 receipts regenerate byte for byte.
- New CLI kinds: `controlled_polynomial`, `realization`, `switched`, `automaton`, `equivalence`, `parametric` and `enrichment`. There are eight new example specifications and a heated-plate application.
- A randomized test checks that switched closure agrees with `compile_family`.
- The suite grows from 72 to 95 tests.
